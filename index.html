One tetrahedron, seven viewpoints

Four capacities sit at the vertices and six dynamics run along the edges. Seen from the right angle, any pair of opposite edges looks like the crossed axes of a two-by-two framework. Seen from a vertex, one capacity becomes the lens and the other three recede into a triangle behind it.

In a pair view, the two coloured edges seem to cross, but they are skew: the amber segment, their common perpendicular, keeps them two units apart in depth. Seen head-on it shrinks to a point. Where edges cross on screen, the one nearer to you is drawn over a gap in the other.

In a node view, purple spokes are the three dynamics involving the front capacity; the shaded teal face holds the three that do not. Each spoke is the skew partner of the face edge opposite it.
